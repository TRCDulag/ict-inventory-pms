Data privacy notice

The records in this app, including the names, positions and sections of our employees, are kept and controlled by the ICT Unit of DOH TRC Dulag. We use them only for ICT equipment accountability, preventive maintenance and job order requests, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

A copy of the whole Sheet (all tabs) is saved on the 1st of every month, at about 6 AM, to the Drive folder "ICT Inventory and PMS Backups". You can also make one now, for example before a big change.

Router setup: automatic downtime log

The MikroTik router checks each line every 30 seconds and tells the app when one goes down or comes back. It sends through whichever line still works. If both are down, it keeps the report and sends it with the right time once a line is back. Lines switched off on the router (disabled) are not logged.

1. In Winbox, open New Terminal and paste this (one time)

It adds a check address for each line (1.0.0.1 only through PLDT, 8.8.4.4 only through Starlink), a Netwatch for each, and the 1-minute schedule. Nothing else on the router changes.

2. System, Scripts, + : name it trcd-downtime, tick read, write, test, then paste this as the Source

It has the app's link and the secret key. Do not share it outside ICTU.
