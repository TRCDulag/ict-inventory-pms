Data privacy notice

The records in this app, including the names, positions and sections of our employees, are kept and controlled by the ICT Unit of DOH TRC Dulag. We use them only for ICT equipment accountability, preventive maintenance and job order requests, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Only ICTU personnel can open or change the records, and every change is logged. Please do not copy or share the data for any other purpose. For corrections or concerns, please reach the ICT Unit.
