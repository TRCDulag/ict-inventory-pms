Data privacy notice

The records in this app, including the names, positions and sections of our employees, are kept and controlled by the ICT Unit of DOH TRC Dulag. We use them only for ICT equipment accountability, preventive maintenance and job order requests, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Only ICTU personnel can open or change the records, each with their own account, and every change is logged. Please do not copy or share the data for any other purpose. Other offices may ask the ICT Unit for reports through a job order (data request).

For corrections or concerns, please reach the ICT Unit.

Every time a line goes down: when it went down, when it came back, the cause and the ISP ticket. The app adds up the hours and the uptime of each line every month. New job order emails

Each request filed on the request page sends an email to these addresses (up to 5, separated by commas). Job orders that ICTU logs in the app send none. The app also checks for new requests every 2 minutes while it is open and shows a pop-up.

Backup of the Google Sheet

A copy of the whole Sheet is saved on the 1st of every month, at about 6 AM, to the Drive folder "ICT Inventory and PMS Backups". You can also make one now, for example before a big change.
